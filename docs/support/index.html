Support

Countdown Dots helps you create visual countdowns for the dates and milestones that matter most, from birthdays and anniversaries to launches, trips, and life events.

Common actions

  • Create an event from the main countdown or events view.
  • Add a name and date; location and notes are optional.
  • Tap a countdown to make it the active focus item.
  • Use the details view to edit, set a default event, or delete a countdown.
  • Adjust the visual style and colours in settings to suit your preference.

Privacy and data

Countdown Dots does not require an account and stores event data locally on your device, helping keep the app simple, personal, and private.
