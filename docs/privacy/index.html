Privacy Policy for Countdown Dots

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Countdown Dots is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal information.

Information stored on your device

Countdown Dots stores the event information you enter, such as event names, dates, optional locations, optional notes, your selected default event, and appearance preferences. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's local preferences storage.

Information we collect

The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share the information entered into Countdown Dots. The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, account registration, cloud synchronization, or third-party data collection services.

Permissions

Countdown Dots does not request access to your contacts, calendar, reminders, location, photos, camera, microphone, or other personal device data.

Data deletion

You can delete individual events inside the app. Removing the app from your device removes the app's locally stored data according to Apple's standard device behavior.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with an updated revision date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the Countdown Dots support page.
