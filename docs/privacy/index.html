Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Countdown Dots is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal information. The app is built to keep your countdown data local to your device.

Information stored on your device

Countdown Dots stores event information you enter, such as event names, dates, optional locations, optional notes, your selected default event, and app preferences. This is stored locally on your device using the operating system’s standard local storage mechanisms.

Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share the information entered into Countdown Dots. The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, cloud synchronization, or third-party data collection services.

Permissions

Countdown Dots does not request access to your contacts, calendar, reminders, location, photos, camera, microphone, or other personal device data.

Data deletion

You can delete individual events inside the app. Removing the app from your device removes the app’s locally stored data according to the device operating system’s standard behavior.

Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the Countdown Dots support issues page.
